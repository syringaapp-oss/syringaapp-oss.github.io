By downloading or using any of these applications, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications are designed to work without collecting personal information:

  • No account registration: none of our apps require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts.
  • Anonymous gameplay data: when you play online multiplayer games, a random, non-identifying player ID, your chosen nickname (if any), and temporary game-room state are processed on our servers solely to run the match. This data cannot be used to identify you.
  • Local data: game progress, settings, coin balances, and purchase flags are stored only on your device and never transmitted to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our free apps are supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect or receive:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and approximate location derived from IP;
  • Information about ad interactions and app usage for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement and (where consented) personalization.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization) or via Google Ads Settings. Where required by law (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK), consent is requested before personalized ads are shown.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

3. In-App Purchases

Some of our apps offer optional in-app purchases (for example: coin packages or ad removal). All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. We never see, collect, or store your payment card details. Purchase status is recorded on your device to unlock the purchased features.

4. Online Multiplayer

Multiplayer features connect to game servers operated by Lily Games. The only data exchanged is what is technically required to run a match: an anonymous player ID, an optional display nickname, and gameplay actions. No chat content, media, or personal data is collected. Match data is temporary and is deleted automatically after the session ends.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Our child-directed applications (Toddler Games, Kids Math, Kids Whack, Kids Balloon, Kids Piano):

  • Do not collect any personal information from children;
  • Do not include social features, accounts, or open external links;
  • Do not display rewarded or personalized advertising; where any advertising is present it is served only through Google Play–certified ad networks in a child-appropriate manner;
  • Comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information (which our design prevents), contact us and we will delete it immediately.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or personal profiles. Anonymous multiplayer session data is temporary and is automatically purged. Local data on your device can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is generally nothing further to delete; you may still contact us at any time with deletion requests.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA and others)

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent for personalized ads. Since our apps do not associate data with your identity, the practical way to exercise these rights is through your device settings (reset Advertising ID, opt out of personalization, uninstall). For any request, email syringa.app@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Third-Party Services Used

  • Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit — payments (policy)
  • Google Play Services — core platform services

9. Security

All multiplayer traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). No personal data is stored on our servers, which minimizes any risk of data breach affecting you.
