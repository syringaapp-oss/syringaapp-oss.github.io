Privacy Policy — Lily Games

Last updated: July 2026

Lily Games ("we") operates mobile games published by syringa.app@gmail.com. This policy explains what data our apps handle.

Data we collect

  • No accounts, no personal data: our games do not require registration and do not collect names, emails, or locations.
  • Gameplay data: multiplayer rooms and leaderboard scores are stored temporarily on our servers and are anonymous (random player IDs).
  • Advertising: we show ads via Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • In-app purchases: processed entirely by Google Play / App Store; we never see payment details.

Children

Our kids games (Toddler Games, Kids Math, Kids Whack, Kids Balloon, Kids Piano) are designed for children: they show no rewarded ads and collect no personal data.

Data retention & deletion

Anonymous game data (room state, scores) is temporary and automatically deleted. Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing to delete per user.
